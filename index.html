Hello, I am a Game Developer

Hi, I am Kareem Elawar. I am a game designer currently pursuing a major in Games & Interactive Media at Michigan State University (MSU).

About Me

Since I began at MSU I have been afforded many opportunities to grow in this field. Working with both Spartasoft and Spartasoft Studio I have made multiple games that have helped me find my discipline, refine it, and learn interdisciplinary knowledge.
In my time working with games I have taken up leadership positions, working as the current vice president of Spartasoft Studio. I have also started a volunteering program bringing game making classes to local elementary and secondary schools.
Games have always been an impactful activity for me. From board game nights over the pandemic to online games with my family halfway across the world, they have been so much more than entertainment.
I enjoy playing games like Middle Earth: Shadow of War, Baldur’s Gate 3, Risk of Rain 2, and various Tabletop Role-Playing games. However, outside of games I enjoy traveling, TV shows, and hanging out with friends.

Projects

TreeKeeper

Team Size: 7 | Role: Designer and Programmer
Responsibilities:

  • Tower Defense System Designer
  • Tower Designer and Programmer
  • Enemy Designer and Programmer
  • Wave Designer

Treekeeper is a 3d, top-down, open field tower defense game where the player plays as a druid defending The Great Tree from the fires of industry. Strategically place natural defenses around the tree to protect it from a fully autonomous corporation that has other plans for the land The Great Tree is on. Move around the map, build up defenses, advance them along upgrade trees and combat the corporation’s legions of AI controlled robots.

The Last Halloween

Team Size: 6 | Role: Tech Designer
Responsibilities:

  • Level Designer
  • Stealth System Designer
  • Gadget Programmer
  • Document Manager

The Last Halloween is a stealth roguelike where a child uses classic toys to steal as much candy as they can from houses in their neighborhood while also paying off the local witch. The player may use various gadgets to avoid the adults patrolling the houses while stealing as much candy as possible. The houses are procedurally generated utilizing room modules with graphics and aesthetics reminiscent of the 90s and 2000s.

Dragon's Hoard

Team Size: 3 | Role: Designer
Responsibilities:

  • Core Systems Designer
  • Spell Card Designer
  • Playtest Manager
  • Game Balance Designer

Dragon’s Hoard is a pvp print and play board game where a team of mages work together to loot the dragon’s hoard. The dragon is controlled by one player and all other players are on the mage team. The game is inspired by Dungeons & Dragons and Casting Shadows. The game is made to emulate the mechanical feel of fantasy tabletop role-playing games with a low skill floor and high accessibility.
